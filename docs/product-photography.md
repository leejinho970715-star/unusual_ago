## Shared prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Generate a square premium photorealistic fashion ecommerce product photograph for unusual ago, MBTI fall Y2K collection. One product only, fully visible centered with at least 12% empty margin on every side, no cut off sleeves hems soles or straps. Invisible mannequin or carefully arranged flat lay, no human body, no hanger. Matte warm charcoal seamless studio background, subtle soft shadow, soft directional studio light, realistic textile detail and accurate construction.
